Mushroom & pumpkin wellington.

Two golden parcels of roasted mushroom, pumpkin and chestnut, wrapped for the table and finished with a sharp redcurrant jus. Built ahead, baked on the day.

Serves
6
Course
Dinner (main)
Cuisine
British
Tags
veganwellington

Ingredients

Wellingtons

  • 200 gAlmonds
  • 2 tbspTamari or soy sauce
  • 2 tbspMaple syrup
  • 1Leek
  • 2 clovesGarlic
  • 200 gOyster mushrooms
  • 10 sprigsSage
  • 10 sprigsThyme
  • 200 gPumpkin, grated
  • 200 gCooked chestnuts
  • 1 tbspOil
  • pinchSalt
  • 3 tbspTamari or soy sauce
  • ½ tspBlack pepper
  • 150 gCooked couscous
  • 2 x 320 gVegan puff pastry
  • 2 tbspOat milk (or non-dairy milk)

Redcurrant jus

  • 100 gFresh redcurrants
  • 4 tbspMaple syrup
  • 200 mlRed wine
  • 1 tbspBalsamic vinegar
  • 500 mlVegetable stock

Cookware

  • Baking tray
  • Large frying pan
  • Saucepan

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 200°C.

  2. Roughly chop or crush the almonds (200 g), then spread on a baking tray. Drizzle with tamari or soy sauce (2 tbsp) and maple syrup (2 tbsp), and roast for 10 min.

  3. Slice the leek (1) and wash thoroughly. Peel and dice the garlic (2 cloves). Roughly chop the oyster mushrooms (200 g). Strip the leaves from the sage and thyme (10 sprigs each), finely chopping the sage. Grate the flesh of the pumpkin (200 g), discarding the skin and seeds. Roughly chop the chestnuts (200 g).

  4. Heat a large pan with oil (1 tbsp) and sauté the leek for 3–4 min. Add the garlic, mushrooms, grated pumpkin and a pinch of salt, cooking for a further 7–10 min. Stir in the chestnuts, tamari or soy sauce (3 tbsp), thyme, sage and black pepper (½ tsp), cooking for 3–4 min more. Remove from the heat and mix in the couscous (150 g) and the roasted almonds. Adjust seasoning to taste.

  5. Lay one sheet of puff pastry (320 g) on baking parchment. Place half the filling along the centre, leaving space at the edges, and compact it to hold its shape. Fold the pastry over, sealing the join with a little oat milk (2 tbsp) and trimming the excess. Roll so the seam sits underneath, tuck both ends, then lift onto a baking tray. Score the top in a crisscross and brush with oat milk. Repeat with the second sheet.

  6. Bake for 30–45 min, until golden.

  7. Meanwhile, combine the redcurrants (100 g), maple syrup (4 tbsp), red wine (200 ml), balsamic vinegar (1 tbsp) and vegetable stock (500 ml) in a saucepan. Bring to the boil, whisking to break down the currants, then simmer until reduced by around two-thirds — upwards of 20 min.

  8. Slice with a serrated knife and serve with the jus and your choice of roasted vegetables.

Notes

Assemble both wellingtons up to a day ahead and keep chilled, unbaked, under cling film. Bake straight from the fridge — add a few extra minutes.

Baked leftovers keep for two days in the fridge. Reheat in a 180°C oven for 15 minutes to keep the pastry crisp.
