About

I am a Software Engineer whose passions include: software design & development, open-source, graphical design, woodworking, and cycling.

This website is intended for me to store and showcase my work: both professional and achieved in my spare time. This is catagorised in the navigation bar at the top with further information available on my blog.

Since then I have been working in the Software Engineering field, on a range of projects in various languages. I am also a certified Scrum Master and have taken on mentoring and line-management responsibilities.
