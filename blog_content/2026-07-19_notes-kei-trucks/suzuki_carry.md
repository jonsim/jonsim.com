The Carry used a cabover layout through 1998, adopted a semi-cabover design in 1999, and returned to an all-short-wheelbase cabover range in 2013.

# 7th Generation

## ST30 / ST31 / ST40 / ST41
**Production:** 1979-1985
**Engine:** LJ50 539cc two-stroke I3, carburettor (28 PS); F5A 543cc SOHC I3, carburettor (29 PS, from 1981)
**Drivetrain:** RWD (ST30/ST40); selectable part-time 4WD (ST31/ST41)
**Transmission:** 4-speed manual
**Notes:** First Carry with factory 4WD, using a low-range transfer case. Two-stroke models require dedicated two-stroke oil.

# 8th Generation

## DA71 / DB71 / DA81 / DA41 / DB41 - 550cc
**Production:** 1985-1990
**Engine:** LJ50 539cc two-stroke I3, carburettor (28 PS); F5A/F5B 543-547cc SOHC I3, carburettor (31-34 PS); supercharged F5A (48 PS)
**Drivetrain:** RWD (DA71/DA81/DA41); selectable part-time 4WD (DB71/DB41)
**Transmission:** 4/5-speed manual
**Notes:** The two-stroke DA81 ended in 1986. 4WD versions gained an LSD and extra-low gearing; rare supercharged trucks joined in 1988.

## DA51 / DB51 - 660cc
**Production:** 1990-1991
**Engine:** F6A 657cc SOHC 12-valve I3, carburettor (38 PS)
**Drivetrain:** RWD (DA51); selectable part-time 4WD (DB51)
**Transmission:** 4/5-speed manual
**Notes:** Transitional 660cc update with larger bumpers. 4WD versions retained extra-low gearing.

# 9th Generation

## DC51 / DD51
**Production:** 1991-1999
**Engine:** F6A 657cc SOHC 12-valve I3, carburettor (42 PS); turbo EFI (60 PS, from 1997)
**Drivetrain:** RWD (DC51); selectable part-time 4WD (DD51)
**Transmission:** 4/5-speed manual
**Notes:** Front disc brakes and 12-inch wheels were standard. Air conditioning was available; agricultural 4WD grades offered extra-low gearing and a locking rear differential.

# 10th Generation

## DA52 / DB52
**Production:** 1999-2001
**Engine:** F6A 657cc SOHC I3, carburettor or EFI (42-50 PS); turbo EFI (56 PS)
**Drivetrain:** RWD (DA52); selectable part-time 4WD (DB52)
**Transmission:** 5-speed manual
**Notes:** Wider semi-cabover body with improved foot room. Air conditioning and a driver airbag were optional; some 4WD manuals offered high and low range.

## DA62
**Production:** 2001-2002
**Engine:** K6A 658cc DOHC 12-valve I3, EFI (48 PS)
**Drivetrain:** RWD; selectable part-time 4WD
**Transmission:** 5-speed manual
**Notes:** The timing-chain K6A replaced the F6A. Air conditioning and power steering varied by grade.

## DA63 / DA65
**Production:** 2002-2013
**Engine:** K6A 658cc DOHC 12-valve I3, EFI (48 PS)
**Drivetrain:** RWD; selectable part-time 4WD
**Transmission:** 5-speed manual
**Notes:** DA63 is the long-wheelbase model; DA65 is the short-wheelbase FC introduced in 2005. Agricultural 4WD grades offered low range and a locking rear differential.

# 11th Generation

## DA16
**Production:** 2013-present
**Engine:** R06A 658cc DOHC 12-valve VVT I3, EFI (50 PS)
**Drivetrain:** RWD; selectable part-time 4WD
**Transmission:** 5-speed manual
**Notes:** Returned to a short-wheelbase cabover layout. The extended-cab Super Carry joined in 2018. Air conditioning and safety equipment vary by year and grade; agricultural 4WD manuals add low range and a locking rear differential.
