Subaru-developed Sambars used a rear-engine layout and four-wheel independent suspension through 2012. Later models are rebadged Daihatsu Hijet Trucks.

# 3rd Generation

## K71 - 360cc
**Production:** 1973-1976
**Engine:** EK34 356cc two-stroke I2, carburettor (28 PS); EK21 358cc SOHC I2, carburettor (28 PS, from 1976)
**Drivetrain:** RWD
**Transmission:** 4-speed manual
**Notes:** Known as the Gouriki Sambar. The four-stroke engine replaced the two-stroke in 1976.

## K75 - Sambar 5
**Production:** 1976-1977
**Engine:** EK22 490cc SOHC I2, carburettor (28 PS)
**Drivetrain:** RWD
**Transmission:** 4-speed manual
**Notes:** Short-lived interim model with extended bumpers rather than a wider body.

## K77 / K78 - 550cc
**Production:** 1977-1982
**Engine:** EK23 544cc SOHC I2, carburettor (28 PS)
**Drivetrain:** RWD (K77); selectable part-time 4WD (K78, from 1980)
**Transmission:** 4-speed manual
**Notes:** The 1980 4WD model was the first factory four-wheel-drive kei truck.

# 4th Generation

## KT1 / KT2 / KT6
**Production:** 1982-1990
**Engine:** EK23 544cc SOHC I2, carburettor (28-34 PS)
**Drivetrain:** RWD (KT1); part-time or full-time 4WD (KT2/KT6, depending on year and grade)
**Transmission:** 4/5-speed manual
**Notes:** 4WD versions offered an extra-low gear. A 1987 facelift added the 34 PS three-valve engine and ventilated front disc brakes on later 4WD models; air conditioning was optional.

# 5th Generation

## KS3 / KS4
**Production:** 1990-1999
**Engine:** EN07 658cc SOHC I4, carburettor or EFI (40-46 PS); supercharged EFI (55 PS)
**Drivetrain:** RWD (KS3); part-time or full-time 4WD (KS4, depending on grade)
**Transmission:** 5-speed manual
**Notes:** First four-cylinder Sambar. Front ventilated disc brakes were fitted; air conditioning and power steering were available. 4WD manuals offered an extra-low gear, with a rear differential lock available on some agricultural grades.

# 6th Generation

## TT1 / TT2
**Production:** 1999-2012
**Engine:** EN07 658cc SOHC I4, EFI (46-48 PS); supercharged EFI (58 PS)
**Drivetrain:** RWD (TT1); selectable part-time 4WD (TT2)
**Transmission:** 5-speed manual
**Notes:** Last Subaru-developed Sambar, retaining the rear engine and four-wheel independent suspension. Driver airbag was standard; air conditioning and ABS varied by grade. 4WD manuals retained an extra-low gear.

# 7th Generation

## S201J / S211J - Daihatsu OEM
**Production:** 2012-2014
**Notes:** Rebadged ninth-generation [Daihatsu Hijet Truck](daihatsu_hijet.md). A Subaru-specific grille, badges and grade names are the main differences; Grand Cab is the equivalent of the Hijet Jumbo.

# 8th Generation

## S500J / S510J - Daihatsu OEM
**Production:** 2014-present
**Notes:** Rebadged tenth-generation [Daihatsu Hijet Truck](daihatsu_hijet.md). Differences are limited to Subaru badges, grade names and equipment packaging; Grand Cab corresponds to the Hijet Jumbo.
