The Every name appeared on passenger-focused Carry Van grades in 1982; Carry Van branding ended in 1993 and Every Wagon split off in 1999.

# 7th Generation

## ST30V / ST31V / ST40V / ST41V
**Production:** 1979-1985
**Engine:** LJ50 539cc two-stroke I3, carburettor (28 PS); F5A 543cc SOHC I3, carburettor (29 PS, from 1982)
**Drivetrain:** RWD (ST30V/ST40V); selectable part-time 4WD (ST31V/ST41V)
**Transmission:** 4-speed manual
**Notes:** Standard-roof, high-roof and sunroof versions were available. Two-stroke vans require dedicated two-stroke oil.

# 8th Generation

## DA71V / DB71V / DA41V / DB41V - 550cc
**Production:** 1985-1990
**Engine:** F5A/F5B 543-547cc SOHC I3, carburettor (31-34 PS); turbo EFI (up to 52 PS)
**Drivetrain:** RWD (DA); selectable part-time 4WD (DB)
**Transmission:** 4/5-speed manual
**Notes:** Standard-, high- and panoramic-roof bodies were offered. Turbo models, front disc brakes and air conditioning were available on higher grades.

## DA51V / DB51V - 660cc
**Production:** 1990-1991
**Engine:** F6A 657cc SOHC I3, carburettor (38 PS); turbo EFI (58 PS)
**Drivetrain:** RWD (DA51V); part-time or full-time 4WD (DB51V, depending on grade)
**Transmission:** 4/5-speed manual
**Notes:** First 660cc Every. Power steering, front disc brakes and air conditioning were available on some grades.

# 9th Generation

## DE51V / DF51V
**Production:** 1991-1999
**Engine:** F6A 657cc SOHC I3, carburettor (42 PS); EFI (50 PS); turbo EFI (61-64 PS)
**Drivetrain:** RWD (DE51V); part-time or full-time 4WD (DF51V, depending on grade)
**Transmission:** 4/5-speed manual
**Notes:** The only rear-mid-engine Every, with coil-sprung five-link rear suspension. Air conditioning was available.

# 10th Generation

## DA52V / DB52V
**Production:** 1999-2001
**Engine:** F6A 657cc SOHC I3, EFI (50 PS); turbo EFI (60-64 PS)
**Drivetrain:** RWD (DA52V); part-time or full-time 4WD (DB52V, depending on year and grade)
**Transmission:** 5-speed manual
**Notes:** Wider semi-cabover body with improved foot room. Air conditioning was standard on high-roof models; airbags and ABS were available.

## DA62V
**Production:** 2001-2005
**Engine:** K6A 658cc DOHC 12-valve I3, EFI (49 PS); turbo EFI (64 PS)
**Drivetrain:** RWD; part-time 4WD on naturally aspirated models; full-time 4WD on turbo models
**Transmission:** 5-speed manual
**Notes:** The timing-chain K6A replaced the F6A. Air conditioning, airbags and ABS varied by grade.

# 11th Generation

## DA64V
**Production:** 2005-2015
**Engine:** K6A 658cc DOHC 12-valve I3, EFI (49 PS); turbo EFI (64 PS)
**Drivetrain:** RWD; part-time 4WD on naturally aspirated models; full-time 4WD on turbo models
**Transmission:** 5-speed manual
**Notes:** Longer wheelbase, low cargo floor and dashboard-mounted shifter. Air conditioning and ABS varied by grade.

# 12th Generation

## DA17V
**Production:** 2015-2026
**Engine:** R06A 658cc DOHC 12-valve VVT I3, EFI (49 PS); turbo EFI (64 PS, on some 2017-2022 manual models)
**Drivetrain:** RWD; part-time 4WD on naturally aspirated models; full-time 4WD on manual turbo models
**Transmission:** 5-speed manual
**Notes:** Lighter body with a larger cargo area. Front disc brakes were standard; air conditioning and collision-mitigation equipment varied by grade and year.

## DA18V
**Production:** 2026-present
**Engine:** R06A 658cc DOHC 12-valve VVT I3, EFI (49 PS)
**Drivetrain:** RWD; selectable part-time 4WD
**Transmission:** 5-speed manual
**Notes:** Updated front styling and digital instruments. Air conditioning, front disc brakes, dual airbags, ABS and stability control are standard.
