Mitsubishi-developed Minicab Trucks ran through 2014. Subsequent models are rebadged Suzuki Carry trucks.

# 3rd Generation

## L012P - Minicab 5
**Production:** 1976-1977
**Engine:** 2G22 471cc SOHC I2, carburettor (28 PS)
**Drivetrain:** RWD
**Transmission:** 4-speed manual
**Notes:** Short-lived interim 500cc model.

## L013P / L015P / L015G - Wide 55
**Production:** 1977-1984
**Engine:** 2G23/G23B 546cc SOHC I2, carburettor (29-31 PS)
**Drivetrain:** RWD (L013P/L015P); selectable part-time 4WD (L015G, from 1982)
**Transmission:** 4-speed manual
**Notes:** Renamed Minicab Wide 55, then simply Minicab in 1981; 4WD joined in 1982.

# 4th Generation

## U11T / U12T / U14T / U15T - 550cc
**Production:** 1984-1990
**Engine:** G23B 546cc SOHC I2, carburettor (31 PS); 3G81 548cc SOHC I3, carburettor (30-32 PS); supercharged (46 PS)
**Drivetrain:** RWD (U11T/U14T); selectable part-time 4WD (U12T/U15T)
**Transmission:** 4/5-speed manual
**Notes:** Air conditioning was optional. The 1987 facelift introduced the three-cylinder engine and rare supercharged models; 4WD versions had front disc brakes.

## U18T / U19T - 660cc
**Production:** 1990-1991
**Engine:** 3G83 657cc SOHC I3, carburettor (38 PS)
**Drivetrain:** RWD (U18T); selectable part-time 4WD (U19T)
**Transmission:** 4/5-speed manual
**Notes:** Transitional 660cc update; 550cc supercharged trucks continued alongside it.

# 5th Generation

## U41T / U42T
**Production:** 1991-1999
**Engine:** 3G83 657cc SOHC 12-valve I3, carburettor (42 PS); EFI (48 PS, from 1996)
**Drivetrain:** RWD (U41T); selectable part-time 4WD (U42T)
**Transmission:** 4/5-speed manual
**Notes:** Front disc brakes were fitted to most models; air conditioning and power steering were available. Most grades changed to a five-speed manual in 1993.

# 6th Generation

## U61T / U62T
**Production:** 1999-2014
**Engine:** 3G83 657cc SOHC 12-valve I3, EFI (48 PS)
**Drivetrain:** RWD (U61T); selectable part-time 4WD (U62T)
**Transmission:** 5-speed manual
**Notes:** Wider semi-cabover body meeting the 1998 kei rules. Air conditioning, power steering, airbags and ABS varied by grade; the Minori agricultural version joined in 2006.

# 7th Generation

## DS16T - Suzuki OEM
**Production:** 2014-present
**Notes:** Rebadged eleventh-generation [Suzuki Carry](suzuki_carry.md). Differences are mainly Mitsubishi badges, grade names and equipment packaging; no Super Carry extended-cab equivalent is offered.
