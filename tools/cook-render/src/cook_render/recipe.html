Burnt Basque Cheesecake

Baked hot and fast until the top is nearly black — the burning is the recipe, not a mistake.

Time
1 hr, plus chilling
Serves
8-10
Tags
dessert, vegetarian

Ingredients

  • Full-fat cream cheese900 g
  • Caster sugar300 g
  • Large eggs6
  • Double cream300 ml
  • Plain flour35 g
  • Fine sea salt1/2 tsp
  • Vanilla extract1 tsp

Cookware

  • 22cm springform tin
  • Baking paper
  • Electric mixer

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan). Line a 22cm springform tin with two overlapping sheets of baking paper, pushed well up the sides — the wrinkles are fine, even useful.

  2. Beat the cream cheese (900 g) and sugar (300 g) together until completely smooth, scraping down the bowl as you go.

  3. Add the eggs (6) one at a time, beating well between each, then mix in the cream (300ml), vanilla, and salt.

  4. Sift in the flour (35 g) and fold through gently — just enough to combine, no more.

  5. Pour into the prepared tin and bake for 50-55 minutes, until the top is a deep mahogany brown and the centre still wobbles like set custard when the tin is nudged.

  6. Cool to room temperature in the tin, then chill for at least 4 hours before releasing and slicing.

Note. A pale top means an underbaked cheesecake here, not an overbaked one — go by colour, and trust the wobble in the centre.
